# Zoo Decision Atlas

This is the canonical hierarchical record of **owner-approved product direction**, seeded from the [ten agreed product choices](../zoo-product-experience.md), [Zoo architecture](../architecture.md) and [roadmap #20](https://github.com/moenarch-games/zoo/issues/20). Topic headings are navigation; semantic leaves are exactly **Decisions** or **Questions**. Preserve each dotted ID. Do not treat an existing implementation as owner approval for a new product requirement.

## Binary implementation evidence

Nested `- [x]` items identify **only the precisely cited implemented/tested capability**; `- [ ]` identifies the specific missing or unverified remainder. No other progress states. A checked technical foundation **does not imply full decision/product acceptance**, and a Decision without a checklist is *unaudited*, not 0%. An issue is temporary execution state; the Atlas is permanent product intent. Use repository-relative `path::symbol` plus tests/immutable evidence instead of fragile source line numbers. The current `daq` parser does not yet index nested checkboxes; see [coding-tooling #322](https://github.com/moritzbrantner/coding-tooling/issues/322) and [#323](https://github.com/moritzbrantner/coding-tooling/issues/323).

  - **1.6** Audience and emotional core
    - **1.6.1** [decision] Zoo's guiding player fantasy is **a personally expressive animal-centered game for an audience including girls** (owner clarification, 2026-11-11): offer **many different, appealingly cute species**, memorable individual animals that players can identify with and feel attached to, and a zoo that reflects the player's own taste. Prioritize **delight, discovery, expression and attachment** over fine-grained economic balance, speed, high scores or demanding optimization. Management and welfare stay real, legible and recoverable, but should serve creative play rather than dominate it. Cute means expressive, recognizable species personalities, readable behavior and visual warmth—not assumed gender stereotypes, compulsory pink coloring or interchangeable reskins. The exact means of personalization and expanded 1.0 species count need further steering (Questions 1.6.2, 6.1.9). ([#216](https://github.com/moenarch-games/zoo/issues/216), [#112](https://github.com/moenarch-games/zoo/issues/112), [#100](https://github.com/moenarch-games/zoo/issues/100), [#119](https://github.com/moenarch-games/zoo/issues/119))
    - **1.6.2** [question] Which **player activity** should first prove Zoo's personal-expression fantasy: designing beautiful habitats/park themes, caring for and bonding with individual animals, or discovering and collecting many distinct species? The choice prioritizes a first complete playable slice, not exclusion of the other loops.
    - **1.6.3** [decision] Answers 1.6.2 (owner, 2026-11-11): **first prioritize bonding with individual animals (option B), then creative building and habitat/park decoration (option A)**. Start by making named, identifiable, cute animals feel alive and worth following and caring about through real behavior; then make it satisfying to design personally distinctive places for them. **Species discovery/collection remains a core long-term pillar**, not rejected or removed; its exact roster and launch breadth remain open (6.1.9). Verify these experiences as playable, emotionally engaging journeys, not just technical pick/camera or placement demos. This orders work under [#216](https://github.com/moenarch-games/zoo/issues/216), using [#141](https://github.com/moenarch-games/zoo/issues/141), [#112](https://github.com/moenarch-games/zoo/issues/112) and [#100](https://github.com/moenarch-games/zoo/issues/100); it does **not yet approve** petting, gifting, player-avatar customization, a relationship meter or specific personality algorithms.
    - **1.6.4** [question] What kind of *bonding interaction* should be the first real play loop: watch/name/discover personalities through behavior; direct gentle care and play interactions; or deeper ongoing individual relationships and memories? Choose a slice with emotional payoff and real persistent individual identity before expanding its mechanics.
    - **1.6.5** [decision] Answers 1.6.4 (owner option B and further clarification, 2026-11-11): **Zoo is now a keeper-first animal-bonding and creative zoo game, not primarily a management/economic optimization simulator**. Players should be able to **play as a zoo keeper**, inspect individual cute animals in real habitats, name them and **interact directly through species-appropriate feeding and hands-on care/play**, with meaningful reactions and real care state. Gentle petting/touch is an attractive candidate **only for suitable animals and with appropriate access/safety**; it is not universal, especially not for big cats or dangerous species. Animal identity and welfare come from `zoo-core`; scene/input/presentation use existing shared authorities. Management, guests, staff, finance and scenarios may support the zoo, but must no longer dominate the fantasy or 1.0 acceptance. The earlier focus on a finished management game (6.1.1) is superseded **as product priority**, while exact survival/timing of its scenario commitments remains an open scope question. ([#217](https://github.com/moenarch-games/zoo/issues/217), [#216](https://github.com/moenarch-games/zoo/issues/216), [#141](https://github.com/moenarch-games/zoo/issues/141))
    - **1.6.6** [question] What **keeper control/camera experience** should support direct animal care: remain top-down with an in-world controlled keeper, switch between park overview/building and an immersive keeper mode, or play mainly as an in-world third-person keeper with building tools? Resolve before implementing a new avatar/movement/control stack. ([#217](https://github.com/moenarch-games/zoo/issues/217))

- **4** Reliability and diagnostics
  - **4.1** Session recorder and failure recovery
    - **4.1.1** [decision] A park session is reproduced from its initial state plus the ordered command log; checkpoint export waits for the versioned save format of #88, with no interim recorder-specific persistence. (owner, 2026-10-17, [#87](https://github.com/moenarch-games/zoo/issues/87))
    - **4.1.2** [decision] The recorder (commands, accepted/rejected results, events, work counters) lives in `zoo-core` and is exposed through WASM; TypeScript adds only browser/renderer errors and the recovery UI. (owner, 2026-10-17, [#87](https://github.com/moenarch-games/zoo/issues/87))
    - **4.1.3** [decision] On a Rust/WASM panic or invariant violation the session freezes and a compact recovery panel offers Export diagnostics (download and copy); the park is never auto-reset. Renderer and missing-asset failures use the same panel with a continue-paused option. (owner, 2026-10-17, [#87](https://github.com/moenarch-games/zoo/issues/87))
    - **4.1.4** [decision] The bounded recovery buffer keeps the last 2,000 commands and 500 events, and exports are capped at 1 MB; these limits are documented. (owner, 2026-10-17, [#87](https://github.com/moenarch-games/zoo/issues/87))
    - **4.1.5** [decision] Evidence is a native Rust replay test plus injected WASM/render/storage failures in `apps/web/scripts/capture-park-view-proof.mjs`; a diagnostic bundle may include one size-capped park screenshot. (owner, 2026-10-17, [#87](https://github.com/moenarch-games/zoo/issues/87))
      - [x] `crates/zoo-core/src/session.rs::Session` with `ZooGame::diagnostics_json`, `ZooGame::replay`, `ZooGame::session_status` and test-only `ZooGame::inject_fault`; the browser recovery panel and `window.__zooDiagnostics` in `apps/web/src/App.tsx` and `apps/web/src/diagnostics.ts`. - **6** Release scope
  - **6.1** First complete launch
    - **6.1.1** [decision] **Historical owner scope (superseded as the primary game vision by Decisions 1.6.5 and 6.1.10):** Zoo 1.0 is a **focused finished management game** with **7–8 polished species including tigers**, **three distinct, winnable/replayable scenarios**, and a complete care/visitors/operations/economy/progression/victory/save-and-continue loop (owner selection A, 2026-11-11; [#119](https://github.com/moenarch-games/zoo/issues/119), [#117](https://github.com/moenarch-games/zoo/issues/117), [#126](https://github.com/moenarch-games/zoo/issues/126)). More species, extra scenarios and a second visual theme may follow after 1.0, but must not be release blockers. Retain coherent miniature-world art, animal individuality and substantive care/layout tradeoffs rather than achieving a number with reskins. The exact eighth species remains a content choice to confirm from playable evidence; the three scenario themes are recorded in Decision 6.1.2. **Owner's subsequent animal-variety/identity priority (1.6.1) puts the numerical launch cap up for reconsideration in Question 6.1.9; do not mistake the earlier 7–8 plan for a ban on a broader, cuter collection.**
      - [ ] The 1.0 content matrix, three complete scenario journeys, real desktop/phone play evidence and final release acceptance are not yet verified ([#119](https://github.com/moenarch-games/zoo/issues/119), [#126](https://github.com/moenarch-games/zoo/issues/126)).

    - **6.1.2** [decision] The three Zoo 1.0 scenarios cover the owner's four chosen themes (2026-11-11): **(1) Building** — establish a sustainable operating zoo; **(2) Habitats and animal care** — improve a struggling or demanding exhibit through thoughtful habitat design, welfare and real staff care; **(3) Conservation** — achieve an explicit conservation outcome through Zoo's implemented care, education and/or population systems. Habitats and animal care are combined in the second scenario to preserve Decision 6.1.1's three-scenario launch scope. The conservation success mechanic is recorded in Decision 6.1.4. ([#117](https://github.com/moenarch-games/zoo/issues/117), [#119](https://github.com/moenarch-games/zoo/issues/119), [#213](https://github.com/moenarch-games/zoo/issues/213))
    - **6.1.3** [question] What must a player **achieve** to win the conservation scenario: education and habitat stewardship, responsible breeding/transfer, or a combination? The chosen mechanic should use real authoritative Zoo rules, be independently testable, and not require online accounts, remote programs or a simulation-only marketing victory.
    - **6.1.4** [decision] Answers 6.1.3 (owner option B, 2026-11-11): the **Conservation** launch scenario is won through **responsible breeding and transfer/reintroduction into a scenario-defined conservation program**, with sustained suitable welfare, eligible animals, transparent program conditions and a durable audited transfer outcome. Education, donations and grant systems may enrich conservation play but are **not mandatory victory conditions**. Zoo-core owns breeding, eligibility, individual identity, transfers, progression and anti-exploit rules; browser UI only presents them. A scenario-defined program is a game abstraction and must not claim an actual external conservation organization or real-world release. Reuse the lifecycle domain [#110](https://github.com/moenarch-games/zoo/issues/110) and conservation hooks [#116](https://github.com/moenarch-games/zoo/issues/116); delivery and winning playthrough live under [#117](https://github.com/moenarch-games/zoo/issues/117).

    - **6.1.5** [decision] **All three Zoo 1.0 launch scenarios are selectable from the start** (owner option A, 2026-11-11). Completing Building, Habitats + Animal Care, or Conservation earns a visible per-scenario completion award and may record best results, but **does not gate access to either other scenario**. Players may play, retry or continue each challenge independently and in any order; scenario-specific technology/species unlocks remain game rules within that scenario, not cross-scenario locks. Preserve per-scenario records across save/reload; sandbox/free-play runs never silently earn challenge awards. ([#117](https://github.com/moenarch-games/zoo/issues/117), [#119](https://github.com/moenarch-games/zoo/issues/119))


    - **6.1.6** [decision] **No permanent scenario failure in Zoo 1.0** (owner option C, 2026-11-11). Building, Habitats + Animal Care and Conservation remain recoverable after mistakes; there is no forced game-over/reset, irreversible failed state, or mandatory time-limit defeat. Neglect, debt, welfare problems, missed targets and slow progress have **real persistent costs, delays and lower completion scores**, with visible diagnosis and a practical recovery route within the normal game rules. A missed timed bonus may reduce score but cannot make the scenario unwinnable. Never erase consequences, silently grant unlimited money/animals, or fabricate a win to avoid failure; bounded scenario-defined recovery mechanisms must be authoritative and exploit-resistant. Players can voluntarily retry/restart and may continue their zoo after victory. Scoring dimensions are a separate decision. ([#115](https://github.com/moenarch-games/zoo/issues/115), [#117](https://github.com/moenarch-games/zoo/issues/117), [#125](https://github.com/moenarch-games/zoo/issues/125))


    - **6.1.7** [decision] **Balanced scenario scoring** for Zoo 1.0 (owner option C, 2026-11-11): sustained **animal welfare, scenario-appropriate conservation outcomes, guest experience and financial stability** are primary; completion speed, operating efficiency and avoided setbacks contribute secondary bonuses. Score components and relative importance must be transparent to the player and appropriate to Building, Habitats + Animal Care, and Conservation rather than a single speedrun formula. An eligible slow/recovering park can still win and earn a meaningful score (Decision 6.1.6); welfare neglect, irresponsible breeding or transfers, money loops, repeated rewards and pause/reload tricks must not yield top scores. Score/progress calculation uses authoritative `zoo-core` state/events, is reproducible across save/reload and retry, and records explained per-scenario results and bests independently (Decision 6.1.5). Select precise weights, caps and grade thresholds from deterministic fixtures and observed playtests, not from an untested fixed formula. ([#117](https://github.com/moenarch-games/zoo/issues/117), [#125](https://github.com/moenarch-games/zoo/issues/125), [#119](https://github.com/moenarch-games/zoo/issues/119))


    - **6.1.8** [decision] **One carefully authored default difficulty per launch scenario** (owner option A, 2026-11-11), instead of Easy/Normal/Hard presets or ranked difficulty tiers. Give Building, Habitats + Animal Care and Conservation approachable, clearly described starting conditions and recoverable objectives, with meaningful consequences but no forced failure (6.1.6). Sandbox/free play may expose optional player-controlled settings, but a customized run is labeled separately from an official scenario award. Prioritize the expressive animal/creative experience of Decision 1.6.1 over elaborate balance variants. ([#117](https://github.com/moenarch-games/zoo/issues/117), [#125](https://github.com/moenarch-games/zoo/issues/125))

    - **6.1.9** [question] The earlier scoped launch target in Decision 6.1.1 was 7–8 polished species; the owner now places **a lot of different cute animals** at the center of Zoo. Is 7–8 only an early playable collection that should grow substantially before 1.0, or should Zoo 1.0 ship that smaller collection and expand steadily after release? Do not invent an approved count before reconciling this with the owner. ([#119](https://github.com/moenarch-games/zoo/issues/119), [#216](https://github.com/moenarch-games/zoo/issues/216))
    - **6.1.10** [decision] **The earlier management-first launch vision in Decision 6.1.1 is superseded in priority** by the owner's keeper-first clarification (2026-11-11; Decision 1.6.5). Zoo must prove a **complete hands-on keeper experience of meeting, naming, feeding, and bonding with animals** and preserve appealing creative building; an economy dashboard, balanced score and three solved scenarios are **not sufficient** to call the game finished. The original scenario/economy work remains useful as supporting systems and historical commitments; which specific scenarios are still mandatory for 1.0 requires re-steering rather than silent deletion or treating the former list as an unchangeable release gate. ([#20](https://github.com/moenarch-games/zoo/issues/20), [#217](https://github.com/moenarch-games/zoo/issues/217), [#126](https://github.com/moenarch-games/zoo/issues/126))
    - **6.1.11** [question] Reconcile the three previously approved 1.0 management scenarios after the keeper-first pivot: redesign them around hands-on keeper activities, retain them as optional challenges beside a keeper/creative main game, or defer full scenario completion to after the first keeper-centered release? No commitment is silently canceled by this question.


## Execution handoffs

One active implementation worker per repository, with sequential acceptance and implementation contexts. Keep the GitHub issue queue mostly flat: deliver and verify one bounded slice, then close an oversized issue as superseded with links to small successor issues for every unfulfilled requirement. Do not close the product Decision/check its whole experience from a merged technical prerequisite.
